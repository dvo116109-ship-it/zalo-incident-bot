Privacy Policy

Application: Zalo Incident Bot

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. Information Collected

The application processes incident reports, sender information, incident timestamps, photographs, and incident status information for operational reporting purposes.

2. Use of Google User Data

The application uses authorized Google Drive access to upload and organize incident photographs and Google Sheets access to maintain incident records.

Google user data is used only to provide the incident management features described on the application homepage.

3. Data Storage and Security

Incident records are stored in a database, Google Sheets, and authorized Google Drive folders. OAuth credentials are stored locally by the application operator.

4. Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Incident information may be accessed by authorized personnel for operational purposes.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Incident information is retained according to operational requirements. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of data may be submitted to the application operator.

6. Revoking Google Access

Users may revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
